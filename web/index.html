WebUSB is not supported in this browser. Use Chrome or Edge.
Dumping can take a long time (up to 1 hour per MB for ROMs). Keep this tab active and in the foreground — browser tab throttling can stall USB transfers.

2. Dump

Insert a GB/GBC cartridge into the GBA, connect the link cable, and turn on the GBA. After multiboot, the GBA will switch to GBC mode and verify the cartridge. Then press:

  • A — Dump ROM only
  • B — Dump SRAM (save) only
  • START — Dump both ROM and SRAM
