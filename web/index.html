GB-Link Cart Doctor

Dump and restore Game Boy or Game Boy Advance cartridges, and write ROMs to GBA flash carts, via GBA multiboot

Neither WebUSB nor WebSerial supported in this browser. Use Chrome/Edge or Firefox 151+.
Keep this tab active and in the foreground during dumps — browser tab throttling can stall USB transfers.

3. Load the homebrew

Power on the GBA without a cartridge inserted, then click "Send Multiboot" — it loads the dumper onto the GBA, which switches itself to GBC mode. Click "Homebrew Already Loaded" instead when the GBA is already running the dumper (e.g. after a previous dump).

4. Dump

Insert your GB/GBC cartridge once the GBA is in GBC mode. Some cartridges have a voltage supervisor that resets the GBA on insertion — placing a small piece of kapton tape over pin 30 (/RESET, 3rd pin from the right) on the cartridge prevents this. Inserting slowly can also help. Once the cartridge is detected, press on the GBA:

  • A — Dump ROM only
  • B — Dump SRAM (save) only
  • START — Dump both ROM and SRAM

Dumps download automatically — several cartridges can be dumped in a row without re-sending the multiboot.

5. Restore a save (write to cart)

This overwrites the save on the cartridge. Make sure the GBA is already running the dumper ROM (use "Send Multiboot" first if not), then load a .sav file below, click "Restore Save", and when prompted press SELECT on the GBA. The file's size must exactly match the cart's SRAM, or the restore is refused.

3. Load the homebrew

Power on the GBA without a cartridge (or hold START+SELECT at the logo if one is inserted), then click "Send Multiboot". Click "Homebrew Already Loaded" instead when the GBA is already running the dumper (e.g. after a previous dump).

4. Dump

Insert your GBA cartridge once the dumper is running, then click "Read Cartridge". Everything is controlled from the browser, and the BIOS dump needs no cartridge at all. A ROM dump takes about 1.5 minutes per 4 MB of game data; padding and erased space take almost no time. Saves and the BIOS take seconds. ROMs are saved at their own size; on a flash cart that is the file that was written to it, without the erased space after it.

5. Restore or erase a save (write to cart)

This overwrites the save on the cartridge. The file's size must exactly match the cart's save memory (512 B or 8 KB EEPROM, 32 KB SRAM, 64 KB or 128 KB flash), or the restore is refused. Erase zero-fills the save memory.

6. Write a ROM to a flash cart

For repro ("bootleg") carts and flash carts. Click "Detect Flash Cart" to identify the flash chip, choose a .gba file, then click "Write ROM". Writing takes roughly 2 minutes for 4 MB. Parts of the chip that already hold the right data are skipped, so writing the same ROM again (or finishing a cancelled write) is quick. Everything written is read back and checked.

This overwrites the ROM on the cart. Carts that keep their save in the flash chip itself (batteryless saves) may lose it; back the save up first. Saves on a battery-backed chip are not touched.

Old data after the ROM, left from a bigger ROM written earlier, is erased too, so a dump of the cart gives back just the new ROM. Untick to keep it, for example a batteryless save stored there. Other games on a multi-game cart are never touched.
